Family Land Map: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

Family Land Map is a map of your family's land. It is made by one person for a family's own use. It has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no tracking.

What the app collects

Nothing is collected by us. The app has no sign-in and no server of its own, and we never receive your location, your pins, your notes or anything else you put in the app.

Your location

The app uses your phone's location to show where you are on the map, which way you're facing, whose land you're standing on and how far the nearest line is. That is all worked out on your phone. Your location is never sent to us or to anyone else.

To draw the map, the app downloads public map pieces (parcel lines, aerial photos, elevation, streams and flood zones) from public government services: NC OneMap, the U.S. Geological Survey and FEMA. It asks for fixed squares of the map, not a box around where you are, so those requests don't reveal your position.

If you record a trail, the app keeps using location in the background only while you are recording. It never asks for "Always" permission.

Your iCloud

Pins, notes, trails and walked areas you choose to save are stored in your own iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit), and shared only with the family members you invite. They are covered by Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy. We can't see them. You can delete them in the app, or stop sharing at any time.

Saved land, survey files and offline map pictures stay on your phone.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
