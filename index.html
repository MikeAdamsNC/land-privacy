Family Land Map: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026 (updated for map copies sent without iCloud)

Family Land Map is a map of your family's land. It is made by one person for a family's own use. It has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no tracking.

What the app collects

Nothing is collected by us. The app has no sign-in and no server of its own, and we never receive your location, your pins, your notes or anything else you put in the app.

Your location

The app uses your phone's location to show where you are on the map, which way you're facing, whose land you're standing on and how far the nearest line is. That is all worked out on your phone. Your location is never sent to us, and it goes to your family only if you turn on the setting below.

To draw the map, the app downloads public map pieces (parcel lines, aerial photos, elevation, streams and flood zones) from public government services: NC OneMap, the U.S. Geological Survey and FEMA. It asks for fixed squares of the map, not a box around where you are, so those requests don't reveal your position.

If you record a trail, the app keeps using location in the background only while you are recording.

Showing your location to family on the land (optional)

This is off unless you turn it on, and each person decides for themselves. While "Show my location to family on the land" is on and your phone is on your family's land (inside its lines or within about 500 feet of them, worked out on your phone), the app puts your latest position, its GPS accuracy, your direction and speed of travel, your phone's battery level, the time, and the name and dot color you picked into your family's shared iCloud space, so family members you share the land with can see you on their map. It updates about every 30 seconds while you move and every few minutes while you're still. When you're off the land, no position is sent; family sees only that you're off the land. It turns itself off after the time you choose (8 hours unless you pick another), and turning it off removes your position from the shared record.

If you choose "Keep sharing with the screen off", the app asks for "Always" location permission and keeps sending while the app is in the background, with iOS's location indicator showing. Without it, your position updates only while the app is open.

"I need help" marks your record so family members' apps show your dot in red and can alert them. Any family member can clear it. Tapping "Apple Maps" on someone's card hands their last position to Apple's Maps app, which is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Your iCloud

Pins, notes, trails, walked areas you choose to save, and your position on the land if you turn on the setting above, are stored in your own iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit), and shared only with the family members you invite. They are covered by Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy. We can't see them. You can delete them in the app, or stop sharing at any time.

Saved land, survey files and offline map pictures stay on your phone.

Map copies (optional, no iCloud needed)

“Send map copy” puts your family’s trails, pins and walked areas (their names, notes and places) in one file and opens your iPhone’s share sheet, so you choose who gets it and how (Messages, AirDrop, Mail and so on). Nothing is sent unless you do that, and it never goes to us. A copy someone sends you stays on your phone until you remove it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
